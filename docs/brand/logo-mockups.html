Four marks for Nudgerow

Candidate brandmarks built on the family's construction grammar, extracted from Parcelrow's mark files (the strongest reference, per your direction) and Vantrow's duo mark. Every candidate obeys the same laws, so whichever wins already belongs next to its siblings.

The family, as built

Read from the shipped assets: Vantrow's V cradles a camel ball. Eaverow is two-tone type. Parcelrow's "Quarter Section" claims one quarter in camel — and its SVG files carry explicit construction law in comments.

  1. One named concept from the product's own world, drawn geometrically on a 100-unit grid, live area ≈ 20–80.
  2. Two colors, ever. Structure strokes in the brand's primary; exactly one element filled in family camel #B8956A. Parcelrow's law verbatim: "gold NEVER moves, gold fills only."
  3. Two cuts. Hero (≥28px, strokes 8/7) and small (<28px, strokes one step heavier, geometry widened for optical weight).
  4. Dark law. On dark grounds structure flips to the warm light neutral; the camel element never changes. Nudgerow's dark structure: #EFEDE6 on #16151A.
  5. Favicon convention. The geometry on an rx-22 cream tile — Nudgerow's cream is #FDFBF2.
  6. Nav lockup. Small mark + lowercase two-tone wordmark + "a Vantrow company" byline (Parcelrow's shipped pattern). Wordmark face per Parcelrow's law: Manrope 800, −0.02em, self-hosted in production (never a font CDN) — this page previews it in Helvetica Neue. Lockup ratio: mark height = wordmark ascender height; gap = 40% of mark width.

One deliberate restraint: clarity yellow #F3DD6D stays out of the mark. It is Nudgerow's surface accent (buttons, highlights, section rules); the mark keeps the two-color family law. Structure strokes use brand ink #4A4952.

The stair is the cadence; the camel ball is Vantrow's own ball, arrived safely at the last step: the reply. Parent and child share an object, not just a color.

The open case question, answered by family precedent

Held open since the palette decision: lowercase or capitalized wordmark? The family already splits the job — Parcelrow's shipped nav is lowercase parcel·row while every sentence writes "Parcelrow."

nudgerow
Lockup case — recommended. Lowercase in the nav/logo lockup: softer, product-like, and Parcelrow's exact precedent. A nudge shouldn't shout.
Nudgerow
Prose case. Capitalized everywhere in sentences, titles, legal — matching "Parcelrow"/"Eaverow" in running text.

Recommendation: adopt both, split by role — lowercase in the drawn lockup, capitalized in prose. This closes the open item with zero invention: it is exactly what the reference sibling ships.
