Rules shared by every option

The family thread. Camel #B8956A colors the "row" in the wordmark in all three candidates and marks the "a Vantrow company" endorsement — sourced from the live Vantrow mark, the same gold as Eaverow's wordmark.

Red is for alerts, not brand. Your intense red #E10600 becomes the in-app semantic alert color — bounces, failures, urgent cracks — where "Fear" is exactly the right instinct. It never leads the identity.

The gray-violet works quietly. #7E7D81 informed the muted tones across all three palettes rather than appearing raw.

To decide: reply with A, B, or C — or a remix ("C, but with A's emerald as the accent"). Second question, answerable from the specimens: wordmark case — lowercase nudgerow or capitalized Nudgerow? The chosen palette is applied to the brand tokens in one commit, and the whole site re-dresses itself.
