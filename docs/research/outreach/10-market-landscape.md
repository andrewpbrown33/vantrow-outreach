## 3. Category size & direction

- **Relabeling is real and complete at the analyst level:** Forrester grades this market
  as "Revenue Orchestration Platforms for B2B" (Wave Q3-2024) [S-MKT-016]; Gartner runs
  a 2025 Magic Quadrant for "Revenue Action Orchestration Platforms" [S-MKT-017]. "Sales
  engagement" as a category label is being absorbed upward.
- **Sizing (LOW confidence — syndicated-research farms, wildly divergent):** sales-
  engagement platform market ~$6.9–9.2B (2025–26) at ~13–16% CAGR; AI-SDR market
  ~$4.1–4.3B (2025) projected to ~$15B by 2030 (~30% CAGR) [S-MKT-020]. Use direction
  (AI-SDR growing ~2× the rate off a smaller base), never the absolutes.
- **Seat-model pressure, observed in pricing structures:** every incumbent now grafts
  consumption onto seats — Outreach AI credits [S-PRC-001], Salesforce Flex Credits
  [S-MKT-008], HubSpot Credits [S-MKT-006] — while AI-SDR vendors price per work output
  [S-MKT-012] [S-MKT-014]. INFERENCE: the ladder is converging on hybrid
  seat + metered-agent pricing from both directions.
- **Consolidation:** Clari+Groove (2023-era), Salesloft+Drift (2024), Clari+Salesloft
  (2025) [S-MKT-001] — standalone engagement is folding into revenue platforms.
- **Cautionary counter-trend:** the 11x affair [S-MKT-013] shows outcome-priced AI SDR
  claims outrunning delivery; churn and trust, not model quality, are the binding
  constraint (feeds doc 12's honesty-as-wedge thesis).

## 4. Segmentation snapshot (INFERENCE from §2)

- **Cheap/PLG:** Apollo, Reply.io, lemlist, HubSpot Starter, AiSDR, long tail.
- **Enterprise suites:** Outreach, Salesloft/Clari, Salesforce, Gong.
- **AI-agent wave:** 11x, Artisan, Regie, AiSDR, Agentforce, Reply's Jason.
- **Data/orchestration substrate:** Clay, ZoomInfo (data), enrichment vendors.
- Whitespace consistent with the program thesis: transparent-priced, AI-native,
  trust-first product for 5–50-seat outbound teams that Outreach's contract posture
  (annual-only, opaque, ~20-seat practical floor [S-PRC-002]) structurally cedes.

## 5. Timing considerations (INFERENCE on observed facts)

- Salesloft's base is mid-integration (close 2025-12-03 [S-MKT-001]) — historically the
  churn-risk window for merged platforms.
- Apollo's headcount contraction (~1.6K→~800 [S-MKT-005]) suggests margin pressure at
  the low end even for the successful disruptor.
- Outreach's own repackaging (Amplify, 2026 [S-PRC-001]) forces its installed base
  through a migration conversation — a competitive re-evaluation trigger.
