# 07 — Pricing & Packaging (Outreach)

**Tier:** P only. **Prepared:** 2026-08-06, workstream F.
**Governing hedge (protocol §7):** Outreach publishes **no list prices** — no dollar
figure appears anywhere on the official pricing page [S-PRC-001]. Everything numeric
below the "What they publish" section is **third-party estimate**, dated and
confidence-flagged. Any public comparative claim we ever make must carry these dates and
attributions, not bare numbers.

Confidence scale: **HIGH** = on an Outreach-controlled page, fetched by us, dated.
**MED** = 2+ independent third parties converge, recent. **LOW** = single third party,
or third parties diverge, or figure predates the 2026 Amplify repackaging.

**Packaging reading (INFERENCE):** the old module ladder (Engage/Call/Meet/Deal/
Forecast à-la-carte seats) has been folded into bundled Amplify tiers whose ladder is
now *AI-capability depth + consumption allowances* rather than module count. Sequences —
the historical core product — sit in **Core**, not the entry tier; the entry tier leads
with AI agents. Note the four-tier reality (incl. Essentials) is newer than most
third-party write-ups, which still describe three paid tiers [S-PRC-003] [S-PRC-004] —
watch for drift when citing them.

Legacy module names (Engage, Call, Meet, Deal, Forecast) still appear in third-party
add-on pricing [S-PRC-004] and in Vendr's tier labels (Standard/Professional/Enterprise)
[S-PRC-002]; existing contracts likely still run on legacy SKUs (INFERENCE).

## 6. Seat minimums, contract norms, negotiability

- **No published seat minimum** [S-PRC-001]; volume discounting effectively begins at
  ~20–25 seats with breaks at 50/100/250+ [S-PRC-002] [S-PRC-003] (MED). Practical
  floor for SMB fit is therefore soft but real (INFERENCE).
- **Annual contracts mandatory; no monthly billing; no free trial** — consistent across
  [S-PRC-003] [S-PRC-004] and the official page's request-pricing-only posture
  [S-PRC-001] (MED-HIGH).
- Multi-year (2–3yr) discounts 10–30% [S-PRC-003]; Vendr: multi-year 15–30% lower
  effective, average realized savings **12.44%** [S-PRC-002] (MED).
- Negotiation levers reported: competing quotes (Salesloft/Apollo), quarter-end, prepay,
  bundling, waived implementation [S-PRC-002] (MED).

## 7. Real-world contract benchmarks (Vendr, updated Feb-2026 — MED-HIGH for its own dataset)

- Median ACV **$45,540**; observed range **$8,500–$213,986**; 545 deals analyzed / 910
  purchases referenced [S-PRC-002].
- Implied: at ~$100–$160/seat list, the median contract ≈ 25–35 seats with modules —
  consistent with a mid-market center of gravity (INFERENCE).

## 9. Packaging strategy read (INFERENCE, for our positioning work)

Land a ~$100 seat, expand via tier upgrades + credits rather than à-la-carte modules;
protect the headline number by publishing structure but never price; monetize AI through
metered credits with allowances that make Pro the credit-safe choice for AI-heavy teams.
Opacity itself is load-bearing: it enables discrimination by segment and quarter, and it
is attackable by a transparent-pricing entrant (feeds doc 12).
