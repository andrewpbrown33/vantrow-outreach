Four ways Nudgerow could feel

Per your ask: these follow no pre-set guidelines — not the family grammar, not the design law, not Clarity Ink's current application. Each plate rebuilds your redlined home (activity feed, date-segmented, filters-as-reports, everything linked) and the sequences overview in a different language. All names/numbers invented; links are styled, not wired. The Signal mark rides along in each plate's colors. Pick one — or name a hybrid — and deck v2 gets rebuilt in it, with the design law amended to match.

Round 2 below (plates 5–8), built from your reactions: Studio's icon chips stay; Mission Deck's live darker feel stays but loses the "codey" texture — no monospace body, no terminal idioms. Target: effortless AND always-on; the feed darker, the rest not necessarily. Plate 5 is the direct synthesis; 6–8 are structural alternatives. Round 1 is archived below them.
